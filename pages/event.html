Ceremony Program

Graduation Ceremony Program

A formal celebration of academic achievement, fellowship, and gratitude.

Sunday, 7th December 2025


1300h – 1700h


Celebration of Eng. PH Magutu – BSc. Computer Science (Second Class Upper Division)

There is no dress code for this ceremony. Everyone is welcome to come in whatever they feel most comfortable wearing.

1

Arrival, Welcome & Opening Blessing

1300h – 1330h

Guests settle in as soft music plays. A designated elder or family representative welcomes everyone, acknowledges your academic achievement, and opens the ceremony with a prayer.

2

Graduation Lunch & Fellowship

1330h – 1430h

A shared meal where guests enjoy lunch together. Light conversation, photo moments, and time for people to reconnect and celebrate your milestone as a community.

3

Congratulatory Messages & Family Tributes

1430h – 1545h

A structured session where parents or guardians share short reflections, a church representative or youth leader speaks, a relative or mentor gives a message, and selected friends give brief congratulatory remarks. All kept warm, encouraging, and focused on your journey and achievement.

4

Graduate's Speech: "My Journey in Computer Science"

1545h – 1630h

Your moment to speak: thank the guests, share your journey at JKUAT, reflect on earning your Second Class Upper, give gratitude to family, church, and friends, and share your hopes, projects, and next steps. This is the emotional peak of the event.

5

Closing Blessing, Group Photos & Celebration Moments

1630h – 1700h

A closing prayer and words of blessing for your future. Followed by group photos, family pictures, laughter, and relaxed mingling as the ceremony concludes.
